### 废案：A Hundred Boring Ways to Work Remotely (Part Three)

Someone asked me whether geniuses need to work remotely too.

Nope. The station's full of my puppets; I just hop into whichever one's needed. "Remote" only means other people are far from me. The one who needs remote work is the Stellaron twerp: he can't even write a quicksort, and I'm still counting on him coming back to test the Simulated Universe.

That afternoon I was in my office, in person. Three screens: the Imaginary Pulse energy-level curve, the monitoring matrix, his comm window. Asta was signing procurement reports; Screwy was still at the relay station, drifting this way at his usual gentlemanly pace.

板砖 was on standby. Give it one line of requirements and it breaks it down, compiles, patches and runs the tests by itself.

Raw record below, unpolished. I'm keeping it so that next time I'm tempted to take a job like this, I read this page first.
