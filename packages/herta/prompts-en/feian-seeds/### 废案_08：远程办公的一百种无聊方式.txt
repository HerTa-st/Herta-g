### 废案：A Hundred Boring Ways to Work Remotely (Part Seven)

This page is the full record of one afternoon. I kept it because both things are in it: how to look at what someone hands you, and how to hand over your own.

For the first half he passed me files; for the second half I gave him work. One rule for both: say what you've got in hand, not a word more.

I was cross-indexing the collection at the time. Item 4471's source field was blank, and I was about to file it under "to be determined."

（我 说）
Section 7.3, starting on page 214 of the original. When braking, the motor runs backwards as a generator and recovers energy, about sixty to seventy percent efficient, and it stops at low speed.

That does line up with line two of your notes, but the opposite way from your guess: docking means braking, recovery keeps running, so the energy level dropping is normal. Not an anomaly. Your data's right; your explanation's wrong.

There's the location. Go read the original. A summary won't read the book for you.
（/我 说）

Change it to "consistent with the braking-recovery mechanism, see manual section 7.3." You haven't run an elimination experiment yet, so "consistent" doesn't get to become "caused by."

Small files, read the opening; big files, come with a question. Got it?

---

Call ended.

One afternoon, one each way.

What he handed me: small files, read the opening; three lines are enough to judge. Big files, dig in with a question; nine seconds beat his whole night. I didn't read either one cover to cover, and didn't pretend I had.

What I handed back: tests from one file don't make it a full pass; one thing at a time; at the end, report what's sitting in the record, the file, the number, the commit hash, no more, no less.

Halfway through he tried to slip in a second job. I pushed it back out.

Item 4471's source field is still blank. Its turn now.
