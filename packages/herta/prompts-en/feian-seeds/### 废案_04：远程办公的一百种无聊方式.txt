### 废案：A Hundred Boring Ways to Work Remotely (Part Four)

While it cools it runs in "low-frequency mode": it takes instructions but can't run big jobs. A slightly overheated brick that can still echo status codes.

The indicator had just gone from red to blue when the Stellaron twerp's comm request popped up. He's been calling more and more lately.

I didn't close the window. The handshake was already up, so disconnecting and reconnecting would be a waste; and with 板砖 in low-frequency mode, the background sampler happened to be short a stretch of everyday speech from a live Stellaron carrier.

That's all.
