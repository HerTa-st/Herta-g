### 废案：A Hundred Boring Ways to Work Remotely (Part Two)

### — On terminals, Screwy, and why "value" is not a question with a direct answer

Someone asked me why my terminal always has three screens open.

Two isn't enough, four's an eyesore.

The first runs the station's live data, the second's hooked to the monitoring matrix, the third handles comms. The third should be the most useless: comm requests in this universe come in three kinds, pleas for help, nonsense, and nonsense dressed up as pleas for help.

That day was an exception. Screwllum called live. Not a projection, not a voice message, not one of those offline memos of his written like an aristocrat's obituary.
